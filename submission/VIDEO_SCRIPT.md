# EscrowPal market workflow narration

## EscrowPal / Evidence into action

A small business has a market brief, three competing offers, and a limited budget. EscrowPal connects the next decision to a clear agreement: compare the evidence, review a Laya recommendation, and fund an agreed pilot through PayPal milestones.

## 01 / Declare the market evidence

Start with the audience, objective, and total pilot budget. Add candidate channels, quoted costs, dated references, and observed clicks and impressions. This fictional bakery example compares a newsletter, a local creator, and a search pilot. The pipeline calculates observed response rates and affordability checks.

## 02 / Inspect the recorded Laya decision

Here is a saved Laya comparison from the earlier run. No model is loaded for this recording. Laya selected the search pilot in both option orders. You can inspect the relative preference scores and decision provenance. Those scores are advisory preferences, not predicted sales. The historical GPU training result is shown with its separate evaluation scope.

## 03 / Review a pilot and exact budget

The buyer now reviews the selected pilot and the evidence. The twelve hundred dollar project budget is divided into three exact amounts: research and measurement, pilot delivery, and results and handoff. A manual change requires an explanation. Creating the agreement records the buyer decision and the analysis digest. It does not create a charge.

## 04 / Accept terms before authorization

The researcher reviews the same scope and accepts the terms. Funding opens only after that acceptance. The PayPal adapter is designed for sandbox authorization, followed by an explicitly approved final capture or void. This recording uses a simulated hold. It contacts no payment service and moves no money.

## 05 / Keep the reasoning with the agreement

The workspace keeps the selected channel, analysis digest, milestone criteria, and activity together. A model preference never releases payment. The PayPal order metadata binds the milestone to this analysis, while the agreement and evidence receipt preserve the record. Real sandbox checkout still needs credential backed verification.

## EscrowPal / A small, measurable next step

Export the agreement PDF, the analysis receipt, and the complete evidence record. Private research code and model weights stay outside the public package. EscrowPal brings evidence, decisions, agreed work, and payment approval into one workflow. A measured pilot, with people in control.
